ABOUT OPS

Playlists, kept in sync.

Open Playlist Sync is a private, self-hosted bridge between Spotify and YouTube Music.

↔

What it is

One local service for two music libraries.

OPS compares paired playlists, shows the proposed changes, and lets you apply only the changes you approve. It supports two-way reconciliation while keeping the final decision with you.

⌁

Why it was designed

Privacy and control come first.

OPS was designed for people who use more than one music service but do not want their playlists, credentials, or listening data sent to another hosted sync service. It runs on your own machine or private server, has no telemetry, encrypts provider credentials at rest, and keeps destructive changes behind review.
